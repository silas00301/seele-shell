Current-note counts come from the open Markdown editor after a 200 ms debounce, including unsaved edits, and update after loading another note. They never scan the vault or change note bytes. Words are Unicode-whitespace-delimited tokens; characters are Unicode scalar values (combining marks count separately, emoji surrogate pairs count once). Markdown syntax and frontmatter count as source text. Above 2 MiB, statistics are explicitly unavailable.
