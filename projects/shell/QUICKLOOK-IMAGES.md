Quick Look opens still images fitted to its panel. For PNG/JPEG with dimensions available in the bounded 4 KiB header and both edges at most 4096 pixels, Z toggles a 1:1 preview (one source pixel per logical display pixel); Alt + arrows pan a quarter of the view per press and pointer dragging pans freely within the image. The mode line sits on its own chip, like the PDF page counter, so it stays legible over any picture. File-navigation arrows remain unchanged. Space or Escape still dismisses. Every file change resets fit and position. Larger or unknown formats remain fitted and explicitly report that 1:1 is unavailable; no unbounded decoder or editable control was added.
