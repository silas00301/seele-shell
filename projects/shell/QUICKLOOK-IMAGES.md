Quick Look opens still images fitted to its panel. For PNG/JPEG with dimensions available in the bounded 4 KiB header and both edges at most 4096 pixels, Z toggles a 1:1 preview (one source pixel per logical display pixel); Alt + arrows and pointer dragging pan within the image. File-navigation arrows remain unchanged. Space or Escape still dismisses. Every file change resets fit and position. Larger or unknown formats remain fitted and explicitly report that 1:1 is unavailable; no unbounded decoder or editable control was added.
